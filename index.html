Neo Life OS Connections

A private connection that helps the account owner and their selected assistants work with Google information and carry out specifically approved tasks.

What the connection can do

  • Read Google Drive. Find and read accessible documents and file information for the owner’s requests.
  • Read Gmail. Find relevant messages and attachments, review correspondence and prepare responses.
  • Read Google Calendar. Review events, schedules and availability.
  • Send email when approved. Send a message from the selected account only when the owner asks and confirms.
  • Make calendar changes when approved. Create, update or remove events only when the owner asks and confirms.

The owner stays in control

Each Google account must authorize access. Sending email or changing a calendar requires a specific owner request and confirmation. Connecting an account is not permission to take every available action.

Information may be provided to the owner’s selected AI tools or saved in their private Life OS or Google Drive when requested or configured. Connecting an account does not automatically capture every conversation, file or activity.

Private use, clear permissions

This app is intended for the owner’s personal use with their authorized accounts. It has no public registration. These information pages do not display connected emails, files or calendar records.

Read the privacy notice for data use, storage and access controls. The owner can revoke Google access at any time through their Google Account connections.
