Data and permissions

Privacy notice

Last updated November 9, 2026

Neo Life OS Connections is a private, owner-use app. It connects accounts authorized by the owner so selected assistants can help with information and specifically approved actions.

Information the app can access

Google permissions allow the app to read Gmail messages and attachments, Google Drive files and file information, and Google Calendar calendars and events available to the connected account. The app can also send email and create, update or remove calendar events with the permissions granted by that account.

How information is used

Information is used to find relevant records, answer questions, summarize material, review schedules and carry out owner-authorized tasks. Email sending and calendar changes require the owner to ask and confirm the action. This is an application rule in addition to Google’s account permissions.

The connection does not request permission to edit or delete Google Drive files, or to manage Gmail messages such as archiving or deleting them. A Google connection does not imply that all information in an account is automatically collected.

Selected assistants and private storage

Information relevant to a task may be sent to AI tools selected by the owner. Retrieved information or resulting notes and reports may be kept in the owner’s private Life OS or Google Drive when requested or configured. Processing is not necessarily limited to the owner’s device.

The owner’s selected tools and storage services also have their own data-handling and retention terms. This notice does not replace those terms or make a claim about those providers’ model-training practices.

No public records or sale of connected data

Connected Google data is not sold. These public information pages describe the app and its data practices; they do not publish connected messages, attachments, files, calendar events or account records. The pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

Retention and removal

Retrieved information, assistant conversations, saved notes and reports can remain in the owner-authorized tools and storage where they were created. Retention and removal follow the configuration and controls of those systems. This app does not promise a fixed deletion deadline or automatic deletion of every saved copy.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app’s authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation stops further Google API access through that authorization. It does not automatically delete information already retrieved or saved elsewhere; those copies must be handled through the relevant tool or storage controls.

Questions

For questions about access, saved information or this notice, use the support contact displayed on the app’s Google permission screen.
